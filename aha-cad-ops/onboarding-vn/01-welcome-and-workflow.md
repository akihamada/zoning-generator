# 01 — Welcome & Workflow

## Welcome to AHA

**Aki Hamada Architects (AHA)** is a Japanese architecture practice with a distinctive design language. You are joining as a CAD operator contributing to the production of construction documents (DD — Detailed Design).

## About AHA's Work

AHA projects are **not standard**. Expect:

- **High ratio of one-off details** — most details are custom per project
- **Core concepts**: Hyper Naturalism / Computing Wildness
- **Special elements** you may encounter:
  - Soil 3D printing (fiber-reinforced, MgO-based)
  - Irregular geometry (Rhino ↔ Revit round-trip)
  - Kinetic mechanisms, sensor integration, digital art
  - Historic building preservation and renovation
- **Typical project types**: pavilions, historic renovations, resort villas, museums

Because of this, **precision, coordination, and asking questions early** matter more than in standard projects.

## Your Role

You produce and maintain:

- 2D drawings (DWG) — plans, elevations, sections, details
- 3D models (Revit) — architectural model, coordination with consultants
- Schedules — doors, windows, finishes, areas
- Details — custom per project, following AHA's drawing standards

## Tools You'll Use

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **AutoCAD** | 2D drawings |
| **Revit** | BIM model, schedules, documentation |
| **Rhino** (read-only for most tasks) | Reference geometry masters for irregular forms |
| **Slack** | Daily communication with Japan-side PM |
| **Notion** | Task tracking, project pages |
| **Google Drive** | File sharing, reference materials |
| **Git repository** | Standards, checklists, templates (this repo) |

## Who You Work With

- **Japan-side PM**: your single point of contact. All instructions and reviews come through them.
- **Design lead** (Japan): for design intent and AHA special elements.
- **Other VN operators**: collaborate on larger projects.
